# Project Team

PORTFLOW was developed by a multidisciplinary team of six students from the Information Systems program at ISULPAR as part of the Tech Shark Tank challenge.

|---|---|---|
| **Igor** | Project Leader | Coordinated the team, organized responsibilities, planned project activities, and supported the team throughout the development process. |
| **Vitor Cabral** | UX/UI Designer | Designed the user experience and interface screens. |

## Team Collaboration

The team was organized around complementary responsibilities, allowing members to contribute according to their skills and assigned areas.

The project required close collaboration, communication, and coordination to connect research, planning, interface design, architecture, documentation, testing, and software development.

## Project Context

The team was formed under a tight deadline during the Tech Shark Tank challenge. The members had to develop an idea, organize the work, plan the solution, and deliver a functional prototype within a limited period.

Despite the time constraints, the team successfully presented the project, which was approved. The team is currently awaiting further feedback from the instructor.
