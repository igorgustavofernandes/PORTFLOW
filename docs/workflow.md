
# PORTFLOW Operational Workflow

## 1. Authentication

Users access the system through a login interface. Authentication and operator tracking support traceability.

## 2. PDF Upload

The operator uploads operational PDF documents to the system.

## 3. Document Extraction

The system validates and extracts operational information from the PDF documents using predefined extraction rules.

## 4. Information Review

The extracted information is reviewed within the operational workflow.

## 5. Manual Registration

The system supports the manual registration of operational information.

## 6. Email Report Generation

The extracted and reviewed information is used to prepare standardized email reports.

## 7. Approval

Reports follow an approval workflow before the final operational communication.

## 8. History and Traceability

The system records operational activities, supporting the tracking of processed documents and actions associated with operators.
